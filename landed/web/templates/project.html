Cost assumptions

Fixed for this demo

A quotation prices goods. It never states what your freight costs, what duty you pay, or what your money costs — so these come from the buying team, and every figure they produce is labelled an assumption, never as something a supplier said.

{% for label, value in assumptions_used %} {% endfor %}
AssumptionValueApplies to
{{ label }} {{ value }} {% if "Freight" in label %}Buyer's leg only — a seller-paid term (CIF, DDP) shows 0.00 {% elif "Duty" in label %}Charged on CIF value, unless the seller clears the goods (DDP) {% elif "Insurance" in label %}Charged on goods plus freight, unless seller-insured (CIF, CIP) {% elif "capital" in label %}Cost of money over the payment period {% elif "Payment" in label %}Days the buyer's cash is outstanding {% else %}Applied where the quotation does not state it{% endif %}

No exchange rate is set. An undated rate is treated as no rate at all — a conversion nobody can date is a conversion nobody can check — so a quotation in a foreign currency is refused rather than converted on a guess.
